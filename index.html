🚀 Today's Core Lesson (Prep 2 - Unit Focus)

شرح القاعدة: عادات الماضي (Used to vs Didn't use to) والربط مع (While / When)
Unit 2 Mastery
1. Past Habits & States: used to + Infinitive
عادة أو حالة كانت متكررة في الماضي وتوقفت الآن
(+) Subject + used to + verb (infinitive)
(-) Subject + didn't use to + verb (infinitive) [Notice: use NOT used!]
(?) Did + Subject + use to + verb (infinitive) ...?
Positive: We used to live in a small flat, but now we live in a spacious house.
Negative: Tariq didn't use to drink coffee, but now he drinks it every day.
2. Comparison: Used to vs. Past Continuous (While/When)
الفرق بين العادة القديمة والحدث المستمر المقاطع
Concept Structure Example Sentence
Past Habit (عادة قديمة) used to + inf My brother used to ride a bike to school.
Temporary Interrupted Action While + was/were + v-ing, past simple While he was riding his bike, he saw an accident.
Present Habit (عادة في الحاضر) usually / always + Present Simple Now, he usually takes the bus. (NOT used to!)
3. Key Vocabulary & Collocations (Prep 2)
مفردات هامة للحفظ والاستخدام
proud of (+ noun / -ing) فخور بـ
volunteer (n / v) متطوع / يتطوع
emergency (n) حالة طوارئ
heroic deed عمل بطولي نبيل
charity organization مؤسسة خيرية
gain experience يكتسب خبرة
